Our Craft

From Sacred Soil to Your Hands

Every Advaitya product follows the ancient Shad Rasa principle — a six-step journey that honours the intelligence of nature.

🌱
Wild Crafting & Ethical Sourcing

Our master herbalists travel to the Himalayan foothills, Western Ghats, and Deccan plateau to hand-select herbs at their peak potency — guided by Vedic seasonal calendars.

☀️
Sun-Drying & Moon-Charging

Herbs are naturally sun-dried under controlled conditions and — for select formulas — left under moonlight during Purnima nights, an ancient purification ritual from Ashtanga Hridayam.

🔬
Potency Testing

Every batch is tested at our Ayurvedic research lab for active phytochemical markers, heavy metal safety, and microbiological purity before the next step begins.

🫙
Cold-Process Formulation

Our Vaidyas blend ingredients using cold-process extraction below 40°C, preserving the prana — the vital life force — present in every herb's molecular structure.

🏺
Ritual Sealing & Packaging

Products are sealed during auspicious Muhurtas and packaged in recyclable materials bearing sacred geometric patterns drawn from ancient yantra traditions.

Tridosha

Find Your Dosha

Ayurveda recognises three primal bio-energies that govern all of life. Discover yours, and find the formulas that restore your unique balance.

💨
Vata
वात — Air & Space

Creative, quick-moving, light. When balanced, you are joyful and inspired. When imbalanced, anxiety and restlessness arise. Grounding, warming formulas restore your Vata.

🔥
Pitta
पित्त — Fire & Water

Sharp, focused, intense. You are a natural leader with strong digestion. When imbalanced, inflammation and irritability surface. Cooling, bitter herbs restore Pitta's flow.

🌊
Kapha
कफ — Earth & Water

Steady, nurturing, enduring. Your calm presence grounds all. When imbalanced, lethargy and congestion emerge. Stimulating, pungent herbs kindle your Kapha's inner fire.
